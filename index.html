This privacy policy describes how Visual Perception Game (“the app”) handles information.

What the app is

Visual Perception Game is a visual judgement practice game. In this release you can play Practice sets with Centre and Half challenges. There is no account system.

Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect personal information from the app over the network. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs in this release.

Information stored on your device

The app stores Practice session progress and a Motion preference locally on your device (IndexedDB / app private storage). This data is used only to run the game on that device (for example, to resume an unfinished Practice set).

Android backup

This release sets android:allowBackup="false". The app does not offer a developer-operated cloud backup or restore feature. Practice/session data is intended to remain in the app’s on-device storage under normal use.

Network access

The Android package may declare an Internet permission required by the app platform/WebView. The app does not transmit Practice or session data to the developer.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share Practice/session data with third parties because we do not receive it.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app. Uninstalling or clearing app data deletes locally stored Practice information.

Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children as a product requirement. Age-band and Families policy selections in Play Console are made by the operator and must match the live listing.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this document and the effective date. Material changes that affect Play Console declarations should be reflected there as well.

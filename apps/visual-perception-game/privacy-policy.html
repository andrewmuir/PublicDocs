Privacy Policy

App: Visual Perception Game (working title)
Effective date: 21 August 2026
Applies to Android package com.visualperceptiongame.app

This privacy policy describes how the Android app currently titled Visual Perception Game (“the app”) handles information. The public app name may change in future; the Android package identifier above remains the reference for this policy.

What the app is

The app is a visual judgement practice game. In the current release, players can complete Practice sets containing Centre and Half challenges. There is no account system.

Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect personal information from the app over the network. The current release does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or account SDKs.

Information stored on your device

The app stores Practice session progress, attempt information and a Motion preference locally on your device using app-private storage and IndexedDB. This information is used only to operate the game on that device, including resuming an unfinished Practice set.

Android backup

This release sets android:allowBackup="false" and does not provide a developer-operated cloud backup or restore feature. Practice and session information is intended to remain in the app’s on-device storage under normal use.

Network access

The Android package declares Internet permission as part of its Capacitor/WebView host environment. The current app does not use that permission to transmit Practice, attempt or session information to the developer.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. We do not share Practice, attempt or session information with third parties because the developer does not receive that information from the app.

Retention and deletion

Local app information remains on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app removes that locally stored information.

Children’s privacy

The app is not designed as a child-directed service. Any age or audience selections shown on Google Play are maintained separately as part of the live store listing.

Future features

If a future version introduces advertising, paid features, analytics, accounts or any other change that affects data handling, this policy and the relevant store declarations will be updated before that version is released.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are published.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the developer contact details published with the app on Google Play.

This policy applies to the current release only. It will be reviewed whenever the app adds functionality that changes how information is handled.
